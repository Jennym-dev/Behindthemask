Ghost is a former special operations soldier known for his intimidating presence, physical strength, and disciplined nature. Mysterious and reserved, he prefers to keep his identity hidden behind a mask. Initially connected to the protagonist through a dangerous assignment, he eventually becomes an unexpected ally and potential love interest. Beneath his cold exterior lies a man struggling with his past and the burdens of his former duties.

Appearance

Ghost is a tall, heavily muscular man with a broad build and an imposing presence. He is usually seen wearing dark tactical clothing, military-style gear, gloves, a camo cap, and a camo half-face mask that conceals most of his face. Tattoos cover his arms, often half-hidden beneath his rolled-up sleeves. His overall appearance reflects his disciplined military background and combat experience.

Background

Ghost is a former special operations soldier with extensive military training and combat experience. After a mission involving a dangerous criminal organization went wrong, he lost the trust of his former team and began operating independently. Since then, he has been determined to uncover the truth behind the failed operation and those responsible for it.

Connection to the Protagonist

He's initially assigned to retrieve her phone and the evidence.

However, after discovering that the organization wants her dead, he decides to protect her instead.

The question is whether he's doing it because of his mission or because he genuinely cares.
