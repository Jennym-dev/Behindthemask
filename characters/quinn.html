Quinn Harrison is an ordinary young woman whose life changes in a single night. When her best friend Mia drags her to an illegal street race hosted by Raven, the masked biker influencer she once knew as Damian, the most popular boy at their old high school, Quinn accidentally records a deadly exchange in the crowd. Overnight, she becomes the one witness nobody can afford to ignore. Defiant, daring and more drawn to danger than she would like to admit, Quinn must decide which of the six masked men she can trust, and which of them she truly wants.

Appearance

Background

Quinn leads a fairly normal life: late nights scrolling through TikTok, and weekends with her best friend Mia, who is always the one pulling her out of her comfort zone.

In high school, she sat two rows behind Damian, the motorcycle-riding boy everyone either loved or feared. They barely spoke, but she never quite forgot him, or the moment he caught her staring and winked. After graduation, he became Raven, a masked internet sensation, and like everyone from their old school, Quinn knew exactly who was behind the helmet. She hasn't seen him in person since the last day of school.

Beneath her quiet, everyday routine, Quinn has always had a restless side: a secret craving for something more exciting than the life she is living. The night of Raven's race gives her far more than she bargained for.
