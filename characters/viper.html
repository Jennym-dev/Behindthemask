Viper is a member of the Black Veil who became involved in the criminal underworld at a young age while trying to provide for and support his family. Despite his criminal connections, he remains fundamentally good-hearted and never intended to become the person he is today. Trapped in a dangerous world by his past choices, he struggles to escape the life he has built. Alongside Chaos, he orders Shade to eliminate the protagonist, a decision that further complicates his already troubled conscience.

Appearance

Viper has a well-built physique and a polished, composed appearance. He is usually seen wearing tailored dark clothing, black gloves, and a sleek mask that conceals his identity. His understated yet elegant style reflects his careful nature and ability to blend into different environments.

Background

Viper grew up in difficult circumstances and was forced to take responsibility for his family from a young age. Desperate to provide for them, he eventually became involved in illegal activities, initially believing it was his only way out of poverty. What started as a means of survival gradually pulled him deeper into the criminal underworld. Along the way, he became close friends with Chaos, although their very different personalities often put him in difficult situations.

Connection to the Protagonist

Viper encounters the protagonist through his connections to the Black Veil organization. Unlike Chaos, he does not see her as someone to manipulate or hurt. Although initially cautious about getting involved with her, he gradually develops a genuine attachment. He tries to keep her away from the dangerous world he has become trapped in, fearing she might suffer the same consequences as him.
