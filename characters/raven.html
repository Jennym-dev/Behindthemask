A famous biker influencer known as Raven on social media. His followers love his dangerous lifestyle, mysterious identity, and videos of illegal street races.

Appearance

Raven has a lean, athletic build and a confident presence. He is usually seen wearing dark motorcycle gear, leather jackets, gloves, and his signature full-face helmet, which conceals his identity. Beneath the leather, his arms are covered in tattoos. His overall appearance reflects his rebellious biker lifestyle.

Background

Raven and Titan have known each other since high school, where they were always compared to each other and always competing, whether on the track, in the gym, or for everyone’s attention. Now that both of them are famous online, their old competition plays out in front of millions of followers, with public jabs, clashes on live streams, and fans eager to pick a side. Neither of them would ever admit it, but each still measures himself against the other.
