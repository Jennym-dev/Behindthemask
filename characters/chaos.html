Chaos is a sadistic and violent member of the Black Veil with a history of criminal activity and imprisonment. Known for his unpredictable nature and lack of remorse, he takes pleasure in violence and intimidation. Alongside Viper, he orders Shade to assassinate the protagonist.

Appearance

Chaos has a sturdy, athletic build and an intimidating presence. He is usually seen wearing dark streetwear, oversized hoodies, gloves, and a face covering that partially conceals his identity.

Background

Chaos has been involved in criminal activities from a young age. His violent behavior eventually led to imprisonment. After serving his sentence, he returned to the criminal underworld and became a member of the Black Veil, where he grew close to Viper.

Connection to the Protagonist

Chaos initially sees the protagonist as a target after ordering Shade to eliminate her. However, his interest in her changes when their paths cross, turning their encounters into a dangerous game of tension and control.
