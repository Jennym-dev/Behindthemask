A fitness influencer with millions of followers, known as Titan for his massive physique. He posts workout videos, motivational content, and occasionally controversial content.

Appearance

Titan has a heavily muscular, well-built physique, maintained through his passion for fitness and bodybuilding. He is commonly seen wearing fitted gym clothing, sleeveless shirts, and hoodies, often paired with a face covering that hides his identity. His appearance reflects his athletic lifestyle and confident personality.

Background

Titan and Raven have been childhood best friends, sharing years of memories and an inseparable friendship. Their relationship slowly fell apart following a troubling incident at an underground street race. Convinced that Raven betrayed him and may be involved with a dangerous organization, Titan distanced himself from his former best friend. Choosing to focus on his fitness career and influencer lifestyle, he wants nothing to do with the underground world or its dangers.

Connection to the Protagonist

She meets him when she needs somewhere to hide. He offers her protection.
