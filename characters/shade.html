Shade is a highly trained assassin working for Black Veil organization. Quiet, observant, and unpredictable. Originally assigned from Chaos to eliminate the protagonist, he unexpectedly hesitates, leaving his true motives and loyalties uncertain.

Appearance

Shade has a lean, athletic physique and a quiet, stealthy presence. He is usually seen wearing dark, lightweight tactical clothing, gloves, and a black mask that conceals his identity. His outfit is designed for agility and discreet movement, reflecting his mysterious nature and combat skills.
